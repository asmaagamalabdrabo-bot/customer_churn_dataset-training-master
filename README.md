# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics and Business Intelligence project analyzing customer churn drivers, retention rates, demographics, and subscription patterns.

---

### 📌 Project Overview
Customer churn is a critical KPI for subscription-based business models. This project aims to identify key risk factors contributing to customer loss, analyze churn rates across age groups and contract types, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

### 💡 Key Insights & Findings
- *High-Risk Demographics:* Detailed breakdown of churn rates across age groups and tenure periods.
- *Contract Type Impact:* Monthly contract holders show significantly higher churn compared to annual contract subscribers.
- *Inactivity & Payment Delays:* Strong correlation between payment delay groups and customer exit rates.
